1. The 6 Core Cost Drivers in Custom Paper Bag Manufacturing

When purchasing custom paper bags wholesale, your quotation is calculated from six distinct manufacturing components:

  1. Paper Substrate Cost (35–45% of total): Governed by pulp market commodity prices per metric ton and chosen GSM grammage.
  2. Printing & Plate Setup (10–15%): Fixed CTP plate charges spread across the total batch quantity.
  3. Die-Cutting & Tooling (5–8%): Precision steel cutting dies (waived when utilizing our 38 standard catalog die sizes).
  4. Handle Material & Insertion (15–20%): Automatic machine-glued paper cords versus hand-threaded luxury satin ribbons.
  5. Specialty Finishes (10–15%): Optional hot foil stamping, embossing dies, spot UV, and film laminations.
  6. Export Packing & Palletization (3–5%): 5-ply K=K corrugated cartons, inner polybag moisture barriers, and shrink-wrapped pallets.

2. Why Unit Cost Drops Dramatically Between 300 and 1,000 Pieces

In commercial offset printing, fixed machine setup costs (plate mounting, ink fountain calibration, color registration alignment, and paper test waste) remain identical whether printing 300 sheets or 10,000 sheets. At 300 pieces, setup expenses account for up to 40% of the unit cost; by 1,000 pieces, setup fees amortize down to less than 10%, unlocking massive per-unit savings.

3. How to Request an Accurate Factory RFQ (Request for Quotation)

To receive an instant, accurate quotation within 24 hours, provide: 1) Dimensions (W×G×H mm), 2) Substrate preference (Kraft vs. SBS Card), 3) Handle type, 4) Print colors & finishes, 5) Quantity tiers, and 6) Destination port or zip code for DDP landed shipping calculation.
