AQL 2.5 Incoming Quality Inspection Checklist for Paper Bags: Tensile Strength, Bottom Glue & Handle Pull Tests

💡 Executive Summary & Key Takeaways

How do premier packaging manufacturers prevent handle detachments, bottom blowouts, and color deviations before cartons leave the factory floor? This engineering inspection manual details our ISO 2859-1 (ANSI/ASQ Z1.4) AQL Level II sampling plan, mechanical pull-test protocols, and zero-defect QA audit standards.

1. Dynamic Handle Pull & Bottom Seam Burst Testing

Every production batch undergoes destructive testing in our internal QA lab. We load bags with calibrated steel shot pellets to 150% of rated capacity (e.g. 7.5 kg load for a 5.0 kg rated bag) and perform:

  • 24-Hour Static Hanging Test: Bags remain suspended for 24 hours to verify zero adhesive creep in bottom seams or handle patches.
  • Dynamic Jerk-Drop Test: Simulates rapid lifting motions (50 cycles of 100mm free fall arrest) to verify handle tensile resilience.

2. Spectrophotometer Color Measurement (Delta E < 2.0)

Visual color inspection under inconsistent store lighting leads to subjectivity. We utilize X-Rite eXact spectrophotometers to measure spectral L*a*b* coordinates against certified Pantone standards, ensuring strict color conformity with Delta E (ΔE) maintained below 2.0.

3. Paper Equilibrium Moisture Testing for Tropical & Arid Climates

Paper is hygroscopic—excessive moisture causes warping and mold during ocean transit, while over-dried paper suffers fold cracking. We enforce a strict 6.0% to 8.5% equilibrium moisture content before vacuum polybag sealing and carton packing.
