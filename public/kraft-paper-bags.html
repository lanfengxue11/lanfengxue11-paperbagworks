Questions before a bulk order

Can I order custom kraft paper bags with my logo?

Yes. Start with the finished bag dimensions, quantity and artwork. Specify print placement and colors, then confirm a proof or sample on the selected paper before bulk production.

What should I compare when sourcing kraft paper bags wholesale?

Compare the same bag dimensions, paper weight, handle construction, print coverage, finishing and shipping scope. A unit price alone does not show whether two quotes describe the same bag.

Are brown and white kraft bags automatically recyclable or food-safe?

No single claim applies to every construction. Coatings, inks, adhesives and handles affect suitability. Request documentation for the exact material and intended use, and check local disposal requirements.

How do I select the size of kraft bags with handles?

Measure the item or inner box, including any protective packing. Specify width, gusset and height, allow room for insertion and test a sample with the intended contents.

Turn your brief into a clear quote.

Send bag dimensions, quantity, logo artwork, preferred paper and handles, delivery country and required arrival date. Confirm specifications, samples, production timing and shipping scope before placing an order.
