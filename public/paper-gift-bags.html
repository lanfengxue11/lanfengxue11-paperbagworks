Questions before a bulk order

Can I buy paper gift bags wholesale with custom artwork?

Yes. Send your quantity, dimensions, logo or artwork, preferred handles and destination. Confirm the paper, printing and finishing specification before comparing prices.

How do I choose paper gift bags with handles?

Use the gift or inner box dimensions and filled weight to guide the choice. Handle width, attachment and reinforcement should be reviewed together; confirm carrying comfort on a filled sample.

What details matter when ordering gift bags in bulk?

Provide the total quantity and any separate sizes or designs. Include the delivery country and required arrival date, and clarify sample approval and shipping scope in the quote.

When should I choose kraft gift bags instead of a decorative finish?

Choose kraft when its paper texture and printed appearance fit your design. A decorative paper or finish can support a different presentation. Compare physical samples to assess color, texture and construction.

Turn your brief into a clear quote.

Send bag dimensions, quantity, logo artwork, preferred paper and handles, delivery country and required arrival date. Confirm specifications, samples, production timing and shipping scope before placing an order.
